### Tech Stack:
Python • Snowflake • SQL • Power BI • Streamlit • OpenAI GPT • Pandas

🔍 Overview

This project presents an end-to-end AI-powered sponsorship analytics ecosystem designed for FIFA World Cup 2026 business intelligence and strategic market analysis.

The platform integrates Python ETL engineering, Snowflake cloud warehousing, SQL analytics, Power BI executive dashboards, and OpenAI-powered conversational analytics into a centralized analytical workflow capable of supporting executive sponsorship decision-making.

The objective of the project was to simulate a modern enterprise-grade analytics architecture capable of:
- Processing multi-source sponsorship datasets
- Generating cloud-based business intelligence
- Delivering interactive dashboard reporting
- Enabling AI-assisted natural-language analytics



🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze FIFA sponsorship revenue across global regions and markets
- Identify high-opportunity sponsorship markets
- Compare sponsorship performance using KPI-driven analytics
- Build executive-level interactive dashboards
- Develop an AI assistant capable of generating SQL queries from business questions
- Simulate a scalable cloud analytics architecture for enterprise sponsorship intelligence

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- North America and Europe generated the highest sponsorship revenue due to mature commercial ecosystems and stronger sponsorship penetration.

- Emerging markets demonstrated strong opportunity scores despite lower current revenue levels, indicating long-term growth potential.

- Opportunity score analysis provided stronger strategic targeting capability compared to revenue analysis alone.

- AI-assisted querying significantly improved analytical accessibility for non-technical business users.

- Interactive regional filtering enabled comparative sponsorship analysis across FIFA markets.
